Use the details sheet to manage one application, and its workspace to work on fit, answers, interviews and replies. Select an application in the List, Board or Calendar view to open it. The sheet slides in from the right (on a phone, it fills the screen).

At the top you see the role, the company and location, and **View posting** when a posting was saved. **View posting** shows the saved posting text and what the job asks for, or opens the job link if only a link was saved.

On short landscape browser windows, the application header and stage controls scroll with the details. The footer actions stay at the bottom. When a newly saved job opens, focus moves to its title; closing the sheet returns focus to its row. A sheet opened from a row or follow-up nudge returns to that control while it remains visible. If the control is gone or filtered out, focus moves to the application's visible row, another visible row or the Applications heading.

Attached resume and cover-letter PDFs must be at most **10 MB each**. Oversized files are rejected before upload with guidance to compress or export a smaller PDF. The list's **Sent** column includes attached files as well as linked documents; the native list names the available files too.

Application Notes keep an unsaved local draft before their autosave delay. After interruption, review recovered notes before retrying the save. A **Remind me** schedule and an application's follow-up refer to one date and note: editing either updates the other. Deleting the reminder clears the follow-up; Undo keeps both until deletion commits. Pausing stops delivery but retains the follow-up date. Times use the account/schedule timezone.

**Prepare application** in Apply generates a combined review of pending tailoring proposals, a letter draft and answers for pasted form questions after one context disclosure. Each section needs approval; existing version guards and account-scoped draft recovery apply. See [Prepare the application together](/guides/writing-application-answers#prepare-the-application-together). Nothing is submitted to an employer.

## Open the application workspace

Select **Open workspace** at the bottom of the details sheet. From the workspace, **Stage, details and timeline** opens this record again. Each application has its own **Fit**, **Apply**, **Interview prep**, **Practice**, **Debrief**, **Messages** and **Library** tabs. **Ask the coach** opens a conversation beside the current tab. **View posting** sits beside it and works the same as in the details sheet.

Saved applications open on **Apply**, where the preparation card names the current step through the posting, base resume, reviewed tailoring, optional cover letter, form answers and readiness review. Select the documents for this job in its rows, open their editors with **Edit**, and download their PDFs from each row's options menu. Preparing, editing and downloading never marks the job applied. After submission is recorded, **What you sent** lists the sent versions, each row's options menu edits or downloads the current draft, and **View recorded answers** opens the saved answers.

Start with [Career and Application Workspaces](/guides/career-overview), or [prepare your first application](/guides/first-career-application). Your shared facts and stories live in **Career → Knowledge**; answers, interview preparation and messages stay with the job.

## Moving to another stage

The row of bars under the title is the stage stepper: **Saved**, **Applied**, **Screening**, **Interview** and **Offer**. Below it you see the current stage and how long the application has been there.

- To move one stage forward, select **Move to _next stage_** (for example **Move to Offer**).
- To jump to any stage, forwards or back, select its name in the stepper.

For a **Saved** job, select **Mark as applied** after submitting. It moves the job to **Applied** in one click and records today's date, the current versions of the linked resume and cover letter, and any answers written in the workspace's **Apply** tab. If you sent different documents, change the links or remove an attached PDF in the details first. Moving a Saved job to Applied any other way (the stepper, dragging it on the board, or **Move to…** in the **⋯** menu) records the same.

On the web, **View posting** opens the saved posting, or the original job link when only a link was saved. In the native app, **Open application** opens that job link in your browser. Opening a posting or preparing documents keeps the job at its current stage.

## Planning the next step

**Next step** shows what this application needs now. Reactive Resume works it out in this order:

1. The next interview that hasn't finished yet.
2. Otherwise, the follow-up you set.
3. Otherwise, what the stage is waiting on. For **Applied** and **Screening**, how long you've been waiting, turning into "No reply in _n_ days" after 10 days without a reply (or the number set in **Settings → Preferences → Reminders**, which can also turn it off). For **Interview**, "Waiting for interview feedback" once every interview is behind you, or "No interview scheduled" before you've added one; the same no-reply nudge follows when it stays quiet that long after the last interview. For **Offer**, "Decide on the offer".

At **Offer**, the offer leads instead: **Reply to the offer** by the deadline saved with its terms (amber once it has passed), **Decide on the offer** with **Compare offers** when terms are saved without a deadline, or **Add the offer's terms** when none are saved yet. When no deadline is saved, a follow-up you set comes first. Interviews booked before the offer stay in **Activity** and the calendar.

A **Saved** application shows "Not applied yet". For a closed application, **Outcome** replaces **Next step** and shows its reason, without an **Edit** action. Overdue follow-ups and long waits are highlighted in amber.

After you contact the employer, choose **Followed up** below **Next step** in application details, on web or in the native app. This records contact now in **Activity** (**Timeline** in the native app) and restarts the no-reply clock for the current stage. For interviews, silence starts from the later of your contact and the last interview's end. Moving between lanes of the same stage keeps this contact; entering another stage starts a separate wait.

The stage's elapsed time and Insights' first-reply figures stay unchanged. Scheduled follow-ups and **Remind me** delivery are kept, even when already due; edit or remove those separately. Logging contact sends no message to the employer. A plain note saying “Followed up” does not restart the clock. In List, <kbd>f</kbd> runs the same action for the focused open application beyond Saved; see [Keyboard triage](/guides/tracking-job-applications#keyboard-triage-in-list). Older self-hosted servers need an update to support this action.

Each click records another contact. If the request was saved but its response was lost, retrying creates a duplicate. After an error, check **Activity** or **Timeline** before trying again; delete an accidental duplicate there.

Completed interviews use their recorded dates. If you return to **Interview** and backdate the stage to overlap an earlier round, that round can still affect the no-reply clock. Review the stage and interview dates when interpreting that alert.

Select **Edit** to **Schedule an interview…**, **Edit this interview…**, or **Set a follow-up…** (**Change the follow-up…** when one is set). When the next step has a date, including an offer's reply deadline, **Add to calendar** downloads it as an `.ics` file. See [Scheduling interviews](/guides/scheduling-interviews).

After editing an interview, the save notice offers **Update your calendar**. A completed deletion offers **Download cancellation** for the same event. Import the file into your calendar app and check its result; external calendars do not update automatically. Undoing a deletion keeps the interview and does not offer a cancellation.

Moving an application to **Offer** shows **Moved to Offer** with **Add the offer's terms**, which opens the offer form on the workspace's **Messages** tab. See [Comparing job offers](/guides/comparing-job-offers).

The mobile app's stage-change notice offers **Undo**. If restoring the stage fails, an error notice says so; check the current stage before trying again. Notice text and actions wrap when space is narrow or text is enlarged.

## Seeing what you sent

For a **Saved** job, **Documents for this job** shows the resume and cover letter you're preparing. After submission, this section becomes **What you sent**.

- Before the application reaches **Applied**, a linked document reads "Linked · not sent yet".
- When you confirm submission, Reactive Resume saves the current version of each selected document. The row then reads "Version sent _date_", plus the resume's Check score at that moment. Choose the actual submitted documents even if you prepared a different copy.
- **Open** opens that sent version, read-only, in the document's history. Select **Back to now** there to return to the latest version.

If nothing is linked yet, **Prepare a resume** and **Write a letter** create one for this job; these preparation actions appear for active jobs, not closed applications. You can copy, import or create a resume and edit it manually without AI. See [Tailoring a resume for a job](/guides/tailoring-a-resume-for-a-job). To link a resume you already have, open **⋯** > **Edit details…** and pick it under **Resume**.

For saved jobs, **Use an existing letter** links a letter from Documents. A letter recorded as sent keeps its application link, and application pickers explain unavailable targets with **Letter already sent**.

In the web and native apps, a missing document can also show **Use _document name_ · Resume** or **Use _document name_ · Cover letter** above the preparation actions. These suggestions use the most recently edited eligible document of each kind whose name contains the company name. Locked documents, Trash and documents linked to another job are excluded. Suggestions stop at Offer or Closed and never replace a selected document or a recorded sent version. Selecting one links the existing document; it creates no copy and does not record submission.

Sent snapshots stay accessible while a linked document is in Trash. Whichever way a job leaves **Saved** for a sent stage, its written answers are copied into **Saved** in the workspace along with the document versions. In the workspace, **Apply → Mark as applied** also saves any answer edits you haven't saved yet.

Recording submission here also updates **Apply**: **Review checks** keeps a read-only record of relevant manual checks at the first recorded submission, without an unfinished count or instruction to submit. Later stage, document or draft changes cannot replace it. **View recorded answers** opens the answers linked to that first submission when available, with the latest recorded answers as the fallback for older history. Those first-submission answers show the same recorded checks; unrelated saved answer versions do not inherit them. Older and imported submissions without review history say **Historical review checks unavailable.**

### Attaching a PDF instead

If you sent a file made elsewhere, select **Attach a file instead**, then **Attach a resume file (PDF, up to 10 MB)** or **Attach a cover letter file (PDF, up to 10 MB)**. Only PDF files are accepted. Both web and native show the first page and the size read from the file. Attaching a resume makes **Prepare a resume** a secondary action; attaching only a cover letter keeps resume preparation prominent.

On the web, select the file name to access the original PDF; on native, choose **Save or share file**. A preview that cannot be read keeps the original file action, and keeps its size when the bytes were downloaded. **Try again** retries the preview. Select **×** to remove the attachment. Private files still require their owning account; the preview does not publish them.

## Editing the key facts

Below **What you sent** are five facts:

- **Salary**: select the value to edit it in place. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or select elsewhere to save, or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to cancel.
- **Found on** and **Found by**: select the value to open the list of everything you've used before, plus common choices. Type to narrow it, or to add something new with **Add "…"**. **Found on** is where you found the posting (Greenhouse, LinkedIn, Indeed, a company site). **Found by** is who or what found it: you, a referral, a recruiter, the app's own job search (**In-app search**) or a search agent you use.
- **Applied**: the date you applied (a dash for **Saved** applications).
- **Contact**: the first contact's name, with "and _n_ more" when there are others.

## Search and Targeting

Two sections keep what a job search and resume targeting wrote about the posting apart from your own notes.

- **Search** holds the **Search fit** (0 to 100) and the **Search notes**: how well the posting fits you before any resume is made for it, with strengths, gaps and unknowns. The date shows when the search assessed it. Tracking a role from **Find roles** fills in what matched.
- **Targeting** holds the **Targeting fit**, the **Build status** (**Build**, **Building**, **Built** or **Failed**, with **Retry build** or **Rebuild** when finished), each reviewer's score from the latest review round, the **Primary lane** and **Secondary lane** (the kind of role the targeted resume aims at, such as Platform / reliability), and the whole targeting note when there is one. The targeting fit is the median of the reviewers that ran; skipped reviewers show why on hover. Select **Read the targeting note** to read it in full.

The fits come from the search and the reviewers, so they can't be edited here; search agents and AI tools connected through MCP or the API write them. Pick a lane from the lists, which come from the lanes you set in **Career → Knowledge → Preferences** (**Edit lanes in Preferences** goes there). Search notes save automatically like **Notes**.

### Contacts

Select the **Contact** value to see every contact. Select **Add contact**, then enter a **Name** and, if you like, a role, a label (such as Recruiter, Hiring manager, Referral or Interviewer), an email and a phone number. Emails and phone numbers become links you can select. Select **×** next to a contact to remove it.

Select the pencil beside a contact to correct its details. **Label** accepts your own wording as well as the suggested labels.

Removal offers **Undo** once the pending request is ready. Undo cancels that removal and reads the current application; it never writes a captured contact back. If the current application cannot be read, the removed contact stays hidden. **Retry** reads the application again without repeating the deletion or restoring erased text.

### Tags

Type in **Add a tag** and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to add a tag. Select **×** on a tag to remove it. Tags are searchable from the Applications page: select a tag on a row or board card to filter by it, or type it in **Search applications**. Clear the search to see every application again.

## Keeping notes and history

**Notes** is a free-form space for anything to remember about the job. It saves automatically a moment after you stop typing, and also when you close the sheet.

**Activity** is the dated history of the application: stage changes, notes and interviews, newest first.

- To add a dated update, type in **Log an update…** and select **Log**. Use **Notes** for things to remember and share with the coach.
- To change an entry, hover over it, open its **⋯** menu and select **Edit…** (for a note, you can change the text and the date; for a stage change, the date). For an interview, the menu has **Edit interview…** instead, or select the interview itself.
- To remove an entry, select **Delete…** in the same menu and confirm. The entry that marks when the application entered its current stage can't be deleted.

Imported stages without dates read **Imported · _stage_** and **Not recorded**, with no elapsed stage time. Use **Edit…** to record a known date; the date field starts empty. Known submission dates stay separate in Applied history.

Dates matter: Insights needs separate known sending and reply dates to work out how quickly people reply. If you add an application after the fact, adjust the dates so they match what happened.

## Editing all details

Open **⋯** at the top of the sheet and select **Edit details…** to edit everything in one form: **Company**, **Role / title**, **Location**, **Salary range**, **Found on**, **Found by**, **Stage**, **Job posting link**, the linked **Resume** or an uploaded resume PDF, a linked **Cover letter** or an uploaded letter PDF, **Tags**, **Follow-up date**, **Time**, **Follow-up note** and **Notes**. Follow-up times use your account timezone. Select **Save changes** when you're done.

If you've [connected an AI provider](/guides/using-ai), the form also has a **Job description** section at the top. Select it to expand it, then paste the full posting there and Reactive Resume fills in the company, role, location and salary; select **Fill fields** to run it again.

## Choosing the Knowledge this job uses

**Knowledge for this job**, in the details beside the workspace, decides what this job's AI work reads beyond your switches in Knowledge. Switch a private group on to include it, such as a clearance group for a government role, or pick another group and choose **Include** or **Leave out**. **Undo** removes a choice. It shows once you have fact groups; see [Use a shared fact for some jobs only](/guides/building-career-knowledge#use-a-shared-fact-for-some-jobs-only).

## Closing an application

When an application ends, close it rather than deleting it, so its history still counts in Insights.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Mark as closed…">It's at the bottom of the details sheet.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose a reason">
    Pick **Accepted offer**, **Not selected**, **I withdrew**, **Accepted another offer** or **No response**. From
    **Offer**, **Accepted offer** is chosen for you; earlier stages start on **Not selected**. If you've added your own
    Closed stages, choose one under **Stage**; a stage with a preset outcome, such as **Ghosted**, doesn't ask for a
    reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">Select **Mark as closed**. Linked documents aren't changed.</Step>
</Steps>

You can also close applications from a board card's **⋯** menu, or several at once from the List view. To bring a closed application back, add **Closed** in the **Stage** filter, open it and select **Reopen**. It returns to **Applied**.

## Deleting an application

Open **⋯** at the top of the sheet, select **Move to Trash** and confirm. The application leaves active views, but its timeline, prepared and submitted document links, workspace, application-specific Knowledge, coach conversations and attached files stay saved. You can restore it after reloading or reopening the app.

Open **Applications → More → Trash** on web, or **Trash** on the native Applications screen. The existing Trash page also links to its **Applications** view. Select **Restore** beside the application to bring it back. Applications remain in Trash until you explicitly choose **Delete permanently…** and hold **Hold to delete**. Unlike documents, they have no automatic 30-day expiry. Linked resumes, letters and their saved versions are kept even after permanent application deletion.

Scheduled application work is hidden and queued work is canceled while the application is in Trash. Restore does not replay canceled work; review upcoming schedules and re-enable any device reminders you need. A stale Restore or permanent-delete action fails if another session has restored or re-trashed the application. Refresh Trash before trying again. See [Using the Trash](/guides/using-the-trash#restore-an-application).
