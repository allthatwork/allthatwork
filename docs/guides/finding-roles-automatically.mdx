---
title: "Find roles while you are busy"
description: "Set a discovery schedule, review open postings and track only the jobs you choose."
---

You have a job already and little time to scroll job boards. Set up a search once, then review new roles in **Career → Today** when you have time.

Discovery reads new search results to check that they are individual open job postings. It skips results it cannot confirm as postings, including recognized closed roles. A posting can close after that check, so read it before applying. The number of new roles varies; there is no promised daily quota.

## Set what you are looking for

In **Career → Knowledge → Preferences**, record target roles, places, minimum base salary, maximum office days and what matters to you. These help explain matches and unknown details.

Add words to **Leave out titles with**, such as **Intern** or **Sales**, to hide found roles whose title has one. A phrase hides a title only when the title has all of its words. It applies to roles from both searches and watched companies, and to roles already waiting in Today.

Enter salary as a full amount, such as **95000**, rather than **95k**. Invalid shorthand stays visible until corrected. Choose **Any** for maximum office days when you have no requirement; it clears a previous choice.

Set your timezone in **Settings → Preferences**. Schedule times use that timezone.

Discovery needs a web search connection. Check **Settings → AI & developer → Web access**, or ask your installation's administrator if services are configured centrally. AI text coaching is a separate connection; it is not required just to schedule a discovery search.

## Schedule the search

1. Open **Career → Today**.
2. In **Schedules**, select **Add**, then **Find roles**.
3. Edit **Look for**. It starts from your preferences; include roles, places or companies to focus the search.
4. Choose **Every day**, **Every 3 days** or **Every week**.
5. Set **First run** and **Time**. For morning results, choose a morning time such as 08:00.
6. Leave **Turn this schedule on** enabled and select **Save schedule**.

The server runs the schedule while your browser is closed. Search services may charge for usage. See [Schedule controls](/guides/career-workspace-reference#schedules) for delivery and email options.

## Check the companies you watch

**Watched companies** is a second search, without AI or a web search service. Most companies list their jobs on Greenhouse, Ashby or Lever. This schedule reads the public pages there of the companies you watch in the Directory, and brings new roles that pass your preferences into the same list.

1. Watch a company on its Directory page, under **Open roles**. See [Watch a company's open roles](/guides/directory#watch-a-companys-open-roles).
2. In **Career → Today → Schedules**, select **Add**, then **Watched companies**.
3. Choose how often: **Every day**, **Every 3 days**, **Every week** or **Every 30 days**, then the first run and time.
4. Select **Save schedule**. **Run now** on the schedule's row starts a check within a minute.

You have one **Watched companies** schedule; it covers every company you watch. A role passes when:

- its title matches one of your target roles (with no target roles, nothing passes),
- its title has none of the words in **Leave out titles with**,
- its place matches one of your places, when you have set any (a remote posting counts as **Remote**),
- when the posting states pay, the top of its range reaches your minimum base salary in the same currency (pay in another currency, or none, doesn't hide a role), and
- it doesn't state more office days than your maximum. On-site counts as 5 days, hybrid as at least 1, unless the posting names a number such as "3 days a week in the office".

Roles that don't pass are hidden, not shown dimmed. When you watch a company, its open roles that pass land in Today once; after that, the schedule brings only roles that appear later. A role that disappears from the company's page drops out of Today. Applications you already tracked from it stay as they are.

A company whose page can't be read keeps its last postings, and the run's notice names it. The next run tries again.

These pages are public, so one checked in the last hour is reused rather than read again.

## Review the roles on Today

When discovery finishes, **Needs you** tells you there are results to review. Under **New roles from your search**, each role has a summary, posting link and match details.

Roles from watched companies say where they were found, such as **From Greenhouse**, show the pay the posting states, and list the people you know at the company from the Directory, linked to their contact pages.

Read match chips as a starting point. **Salary not listed** or **Office days not stated** means the posting did not establish that detail. It is a question to resolve, not confirmation that your preference is met.

For each role:

- **Read posting** to inspect the job before deciding.
- **Track in Applications** to create a Saved application with its posting and match notes. A role from a watched company also brings its full text, pay, location, company and where it was found (such as Greenhouse). This does not submit an application.
- **Not for me** to dismiss it. Use **Undo** if you change your mind.

Once tracked, [check Fit](/guides/checking-job-fit) using your experience, then prepare the application.

## Adjust or pause the search

Select a schedule's row to edit its search, frequency or time. Use its switch to pause it without losing those settings. Delete it when you no longer need it; the notice offers **Undo**.

Pausing or changing a schedule cancels its old work still in progress. A cancelled run does not publish a briefing or notification.

Untracked postings found again are rechecked after a week. Changed descriptions or recognized closures update the existing role without another new-role notice.

Changing your preferences updates how role details are matched. Review the schedule's **Look for** text separately when your search direction changes; it was copied from preferences when you created the schedule.

Each run has a time limit for its search and for reading the postings it finds: 90 seconds by default, and each web search or page read within it 20 seconds. Raise them in **Settings → AI & developer → Web access → Time limits** when runs stop before reading every posting or your search service is slow.

If nothing new appears, check that the schedule is on and when it last ran. A completed search can have no new usable postings. For a failure notice, check the web connection and [troubleshooting](/guides/career-workspace-reference#when-something-does-not-work).
