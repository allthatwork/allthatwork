description: "Keep every job you apply for in one place: see what needs you next, move applications through stages, and switch between Board, List, Insights and Calendar."

**Applications** is where you keep track of the jobs you're going for. Each application holds the role, the company, the saved job posting, the resume and letter you sent, your contacts, notes and a dated history. It also works out what each application needs next, such as an interview coming up or a follow-up that's due.

For focused work on one job, open its page and choose a **Work on it** row. [Career and Application Workspaces](/guides/career-overview) explains Fit, Apply, interview preparation, practice, replies and saved results. Shared experience and search schedules live under **Career**.

To open it, select **Applications** in the sidebar (on a phone, it's in the bottom tab bar).

Their timezone follows **Settings → Preferences**.

## How stages work

Every application sits in one stage. The first five stages form a pipeline you move through in order:

| Stage         | Use it when                                                              |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Saved**     | You found the job but haven't applied yet.                               |
| **Applied**   | You sent your application.                                               |
| **Screening** | A recruiter or hiring manager got back to you, or you have a first call. |
| **Interview** | You're in the interview process.                                         |
| **Offer**     | You received an offer.                                                   |
| **Closed**    | The application ended, whatever the outcome.                             |

You can close an application from any stage. Closing asks for a reason (**Accepted offer**, **Not selected**, **I withdrew**, **Accepted another offer** or **No response**), which Insights uses later. Closing from **Offer** starts on **Accepted offer**. Closed applications are hidden until you add **Closed** in the **Stage** filter (see [Finding applications](#finding-applications)). See [Managing an application](/guides/managing-an-application#closing-an-application).

<Note>
  When an application with a linked resume or letter reaches **Applied** or a later stage, Reactive Resume saves a copy
  of each as a "Sent to *company*" version. You can always open exactly what you sent, even after you keep editing the
  document.
</Note>

## Choosing a view

Use the tabs beside the **Applications** title to switch views. Your choice is kept in the page address, so a bookmark opens the same view. On a narrower window the tabs show their icons only.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Board">
    The default view. One column per stage, with a card for each application, in the order set by [Sort](#sorting). A card shows the search fit (`Fit NN%`) and the resume **Build** status next to the next step; select **Build** on a card to queue or unqueue it for a targeted resume build. Drag a card to another column to move it to that stage. Each card also has a **⋯** menu with **Move to…**, **Close…** and **Delete…**, which is handy if you prefer the keyboard. The columns follow the **Stage** filter, so with **Closed** included a **Closed** column appears too, and with one stage chosen you get one column on the left. Dragging a card there asks why it ended before closing it, so Insights can use the outcome.

    The columns share the page's width evenly (never wider than with five stages showing) and the board fills the window's height, with each column scrolling on its own. To make them wider or narrower, drag the line in the gap after any column: every column takes that width, from 256 to 640 pixels, and it's saved to your account. Double-click the line to go back to sharing the width evenly. When the columns don't fit, the board scrolls sideways; a fade on the edge shows there are more columns that way. Dragging a card toward the edge scrolls the board too.

    - Select a group heading to collapse or expand it.
    - Select a column heading to sort within each group by that column (Role, Next step, Fit, Targeting fit, Applied, Added and Updated sort). Select it again to reverse the order. It's the same sort as the **Sort** button.
    - Choose the columns with **Columns** at the end of the toolbar: tick a column to show it, untick it to hide it, drag the grips to change the order, or select **Reset** for the default columns. **Role** always comes first. Besides the default **Next step**, **Fit**, **Build**, **Stage**, **Sent** and **Updated**, you can show **Targeting fit**, **Found by**, **Found on**, **Lane**, **Salary**, **Tags**, **Follow-up**, **Applied** (the date you sent it) and **Added** (the date you saved it).
    - Drag the line between two headings to make the column after it wider or narrower; **Role** takes whatever width is left. Double-click the line to put that column back to its starting width. Your columns, their order and their widths are saved to your account, so they're the same in any browser.
    - The **Next step** column shows the next interview, the follow-up you set, or how long you've been waiting. It turns amber when something is overdue or you've had no reply for 10 days or more (you can change the number of days or turn this off in **Settings → Preferences → Reminders**).
    - The **Sent** column shows an icon for a linked resume and a linked letter. It says **None** in amber when an application was sent without a linked document.
    - Select a row to open its details.

### Sorting

**Sort** at the end of the toolbar orders the cards in each Board column and the rows in each List group by **Updated**, **Added**, **Applied**, **Next step**, **Fit** (the search fit), **Targeting fit**, **Role** or **Company**. Below the fields, pick the direction, worded for the field: **Newest first** or **Oldest first** for dates, **Highest first** or **Lowest first** for fits, **Soonest first** or **Latest first** for the next step, **A to Z** or **Z to A** for names. Applications without a value, such as no fit yet, go last either way.

Your sort is saved to your account, so the page opens with it anywhere. It's also in the page address, so a link with a sort opens with that sort. Calendar and Insights don't use it.

On phones, the page shows **List**, **Insights** and **Calendar** (it opens on List; Calendar lists upcoming interviews by day, see [Scheduling interviews](/guides/scheduling-interviews#in-a-phone-browser)), and list rows show the company and next step on a second line. **Sort** is the icon button after **Filters**; the List's columns can't be changed there.

## Keyboard triage in List

In the web app, use <kbd>Tab</kbd> to focus an application's role button. Its outline shows which application receives shortcuts. Moving focus outside the row stops bare-key triage.

| Key                         | Action                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <kbd>j</kbd> / <kbd>k</kbd> | Focus the next / previous displayed application. Stops at the ends; follows sorting, filters and expanded groups.                                                                                    |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd>            | Open the focused application's details.                                                                                                                                                              |
| <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>5</kbd>   | Move to **Saved**, **Applied**, **Screening**, **Interview** or **Offer**, respectively, using that category's default stage. **Undo** cancels the pending move; a newer stage saved elsewhere wins. |
| <kbd>x</kbd>                | Toggle the focused application in the existing bulk selection.                                                                                                                                       |
| <kbd>f</kbd>                | Record **Followed up** for an open application beyond Saved. Sends no message; see [Managing an application](/guides/managing-an-application).                                                       |

Typing in a field or editor, composing text, modifier chords, and open dialogs, menus or sheets do not trigger background triage. Holding a command key does not repeat stage, selection or contact actions. A hidden or removed application cannot receive the next action.

## Finding applications

Type in **Search applications** to filter as you type. Search looks at the role, company, location, contact names and tags.

After the search box there's a button for each filter. Select one to open it, tick or type what you want, and the page shows only the applications that match. Within one filter, ticked values mean "any of these"; different filters combine, so **Build: Built** and **Found by: Dorel** shows built applications that Dorel found.

| Filter       | Shows applications                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Stage**    | In the stages you tick. It starts at the five open stages ("Stage: Open"); add **Closed** to see closed ones. **All stages** ticks all six.                                                                                                        |
| **Build**    | By resume build status: **Not queued**, **Queued**, **Building**, **Built** or **Failed**.                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Found by** | Found by who or what you tick, or **Not set**.                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Lane**     | Aimed at the lanes you tick (your lanes from **Preferences**, plus any others in use), or **No lane**. It matches either lane unless you pick **Primary only**.                                                                                    |
| **Fit**      | With a search fit, targeting fit or one reviewer's fit (**A**, **B**, **C**… from the latest review, each named with its model) of at least the number you type after **≥** (75 means 75 and up). **any** (empty) includes the ones without a fit. |
| **Dates**    | Added or updated in the last so many days: type a number from 1 to 365.                                                                                                                                                                            |

Fit and Dates filter as you type. The number at the right of a filled row says how many applications show with it, Up and Down step by 1 (Shift with them by 5), and Enter or Esc closes the filter. A field left empty shows **any**.

**More filters** adds four more: **Found on**, **Documents** (a linked resume or cover letter, or none), **Needs attention** (no reply for a while, a follow-up that's due, an interview coming, questions to answer) and **Tags**. In Tags, select **+** for a tag the application must have and **−** for one it must not have; with two or more **+** tags, choose **Any of** or **All of** them.

- Each option shows how many applications you'd get if you picked it, given the other filters. Options at 0 stay in the list, greyed.
- A set filter turns blue and shows its value. Select its **×** to clear just that one. **Clear all**, at the end of the row, clears every filter and the search, and says how many applications show out of the total.
- The filters are kept in the page address, so reloading, going back, a bookmark or a copied link keeps them.
- The browser tab remembers its filters too: going to **Applications** from the sidebar, or back from an application's workspace, brings back the filters you last used in that tab, and the saved view if one was on. A new tab opens your default view (see [Saved views](#saved-views)), or the open stages only without one.
- If you change an application so it no longer matches (say you select **Build** on a card while showing **Build: Not queued**), it stays where it is, faded, with "Doesn't match the filters now", until you change the filters or reload. That way you can work through a filtered list without cards jumping away.
- If nothing matches, select **Clear filters**.
- When the window can't hold the search box, the filters, and Sort and Columns on one line, the toolbar takes two: the search box with Sort and Columns, then all the filters.
- On a phone, select **Filters** to open them all in one sheet. The filters you set show as chips under the search box, each with an **×**.

The Board, List and Calendar follow the filters, and **Export to CSV** exports what they show. Insights always counts every application, so it hides the search and the filters.

## Saved views

A saved view is a name for a set of filters, a sort and the List's columns, so a combination you use often, like "Ready to apply" or "In flight", is one click away. **Views**, beside the tabs at the top of the page, opens the menu: **All applications**, then your views, each with how many applications it matches right now.

- **Picking a view** sets its filters, its sort and its List columns, and empties the search box. The button turns blue and reads "View: Ready to apply". Its **×**, or **All applications** in the menu, leaves the view and puts back your own sort and columns.
- **All applications** is the page as it is without views: the open stages, no other filters, and your own sort and columns, which save to your account as you change them.
- **Edited**: change a filter, the sort or the columns while a view is on, and the button adds "(edited)". Nothing changes the saved view until you select **Update "name"** in the menu (the toast has **Undo**). **Discard changes** puts the view back as it was saved. **Clear all** clears the filters and keeps you in the view, edited. The search text never counts.
- **Save as new view…** asks for a name (up to 60 characters; names are unique, ignoring case) and shows what the view keeps. The new view goes at the end of the list and is on straight away. Tick **Open Applications with this view** to make it your default.
- **Your default view**, marked with a star, is what a new browser tab opens with. A tab you've already used keeps its last filters and view, and a link with filters in it opens those filters with no view on.
- **Column widths** are yours in every view: drag a column wider once and it's wider everywhere. A view keeps which columns show and their order.
- **Manage views…** opens a panel where you drag the grips to reorder your views, select the pencil to rename one (Enter saves, Esc cancels), the star to set or clear the default, and the bin to delete one (the toast has **Undo**). Changes save as you make them. You can keep up to 50 views.

The Board and Calendar follow a view's filters, and the Board its sort too; the columns only matter on the List. Insights hides **Views**, as it hides the filters. On phones, **Views** sits at the end of the tabs' line and opens a sheet with the same menu; phones have no column picker, so a view's columns only show on a wider window. Your views are in **Export everything** and come back with the account import.

Press <kbd>/</kbd> with an Applications view focused, outside a text field, to focus search. Open sheets and alerts keep keyboard focus.
Rows and board cards show up to two of an application's tags, with a count for the rest. Select a tag to add it to the **Tags** filter without leaving the list.

## Acting on several applications at once

In the List view on a computer or tablet, select the checkboxes next to the applications you want. The checkbox in the table header selects every row in the expanded stages, or clears them; it shows a partial state while only some are selected. After you select at least one row, a bar appears at the bottom of the page with:

- **Select all** to add every matching application in expanded stages
- **Tags…** to add the same tag to each
- **Move to…** to move them all to one stage
- **Close…** to close them all with one reason
- **Clear selection** (×) to deselect
- **Move to Trash** to move them to Trash, where they stay for 30 days

Bulk moves and keyboard triage show the new stage and **Undo** once the pending move is ready. They're saved when the message goes away, or when you reload or close the tab; **Undo** puts the selected applications back where they were, without recording a stage change. If you move one of them again before then, here or on another device, that later move stays, even if it moved away and back to the same stage: the bulk move leaves it and says so, and **Undo** leaves it too. Other edits, such as a note, don't stop the move. If you move the same applications again before the first move is saved, or while a move of one of them from its own menu or details, **Mark as applied**, or a message's changes are still being saved, the later move is saved after the earlier one, so it's the one that stays. Tags and moves to Trash are saved straight away.

You can act on up to 200 applications at once. Larger selections show a reminder; deselect rows or narrow the search before choosing an action. The list leaves room to scroll its final rows above the selection bar, including when its buttons wrap.

Selection follows the visible rows. Filtering out a stage, collapsing a stage or changing the search removes hidden selections before a bulk action.

## Follow-up reminders

A reminder appears above the list when applications owe a follow-up: the follow-up date you set is due, an application has been at **Applied** or **Screening** for 10 days without a reply, or 10 days have passed since its last recorded interview ended. Ten days is the default; your own number from **Settings → Preferences → Reminders** applies everywhere.

- With one, the reminder names it, for example "Harborlight Labs: no reply for 17 days." Select **Open** to go to that application.
- With several, it counts them, for example "8 applications need a follow-up." Select **Show** to filter the list to them (the **Needs attention** filter, set to late replies and due follow-ups); **Clear all** returns to the full list.

Select **×** to dismiss the reminder. Dismissing is remembered on this device, so those applications don't come back in it here. The same rules feed **Needs you** in [Today](/guides/career-overview), which also lists interviews in the next week and offers to decide.

To wait longer before the reminder, or to stop these reminders altogether, go to **Settings → Preferences → Reminders**. See [Choose your reminders](/guides/changing-appearance-and-language#choose-your-reminders).

## Opening Applications from anywhere

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows and Linux) and type a company or role to find an application from the first list, or choose **Applications** to go to the page. Run **Save job** to open the add dialog. See [Using the command bar](/guides/using-the-command-bar).
