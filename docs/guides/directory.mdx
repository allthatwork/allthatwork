Open **Directory** in the sidebar to see **Companies** and **Contacts**. These records belong to your account and stay available across devices.

Both tabs are compact lists, A to Z, under letter headers that stay pinned while you scroll. The rail on the right jumps to a letter; grey letters have nobody under them. Names that start with a digit or another script go under **#**, after Z. The toolbar shows the total, and search filters the rows while keeping the letters. On a phone each row takes two short lines.

## Companies

A company is created when you track an application. Names that differ only in capitalization or spacing use the same company. You can also select **Add company** to keep a company before applying there.

Each row shows the company's logo, name and domain, how many contacts and applications it has, **Jobs** and **Watch**. Companies sort by name, ignoring case and accents.

- **Jobs** shows how many roles are open and a **Jobs ↗** button that opens the company's jobs page in a new tab: the page you watch, or else one a posting link on file points at. A link saved from a site that can't be watched, such as Workday, gets **Jobs ↗** with no count. A dash means no jobs page is known yet.
- **Watch** is a checkbox, and the only place the list says whether you watch a company. Tick it to watch the page found from a posting link; a message confirms it with **Undo**. Untick it to stop watching. It's greyed out when no jobs page is known (open the company to look one up) or when the company's saved link isn't on Greenhouse, Ashby or Lever; hover over it to see which.

Selecting **Jobs ↗** or the checkbox doesn't open the company; the rest of the row does. On a phone the checkbox sits at the right, and the second line starts with the open count and **Jobs ↗**.

Open a company to see its contacts, A to Z by last name (the first five, then **Show more**), and its applications, newest first, with their stage and date. **Edit company** lets you change its name, website and notes. A website's icon appears as its logo when available. Renaming a company also updates the company name on its linked applications.

### Watch a company's open roles

Most companies list their jobs on Greenhouse, Ashby or Lever. A company page's **Open roles** section watches the company's page there, so the [Watched companies schedule](/guides/finding-roles-automatically#check-the-companies-you-watch) brings its new roles that match your Preferences into **Career → Today**. Nothing is watched until you confirm.

- **Found on Greenhouse · from a posting link on file**: one of the company's applications links to a posting there. Select **Confirm**.
- **Paste the company's careers link**, then **Look up**. A posting link works, and so does a careers page that links to or embeds its jobs. A link that doesn't lead to Greenhouse, Ashby or Lever (Workday, iCIMS, or a careers page that builds its list with scripts) is saved as the company's jobs link instead, with a note that it won't be watched. It then shows as **Saved link** with **Jobs ↗**, and its Watch box stays greyed out. A company has one saved link: pasting another replaces it, and watching a page clears it.
- **Find it for me** tries names made from the company's name. Each result shows the name the platform gives, where it gives one, its number of open roles and a few titles. A guess can land on another company's jobs, so check before selecting **Confirm**.

A watched row reads **Watching on Greenhouse · 143 open · checked 2 hours ago**, with **Open on Greenhouse** and **Stop watching**. A company can be watched in more than one place. **Stop watching** removes one; roles it already found stay in Today until you dismiss them or they close. When the company has no website, its postings can fill it in; a website you set is never replaced.

On the **Companies** tab, **Recommended companies to watch** is one line with the three companies where you know the most people. **Show all** opens the full list in place; where a posting link on file names the company's jobs page, it shows how many open roles match your preferences. Open one to find its careers page, then tick **Watch**.

## Contacts

Select **Add contact** to save a first and last name, position, optional company, profile links, email addresses, phone numbers, tags, notes, and how you know them. Use one line per email, phone number, profile link or tag.

Contacts sort and group by last name, then first name, ignoring case and accents. Anything after a comma in the last name (credentials, as in "Menon, PMP") and anything in parentheses is left out of the sort, and the part they sort by shows in bold. People who use one name, and contacts saved before first and last name were separate, go in **No last name** at the end, sorted by first name. Editing a contact, or importing from LinkedIn again, fills in the last name. Names you type are saved as typed.

Each row shows the name, the position with **Looking for work** at its start, the company and the last contact date. Select a row to open the contact, or the company to open the company. **Delete all contacts** is in the **⋯** menu next to **Add contact**.

A contact's company is where they work. Keep an agency recruiter under their agency, even when they help with an application at another company. **No company** is available for people between jobs or other personal contacts. **Looking for work** is a separate choice.

A contact page shows linked applications and interviews together with manual notes. You can also record **Connected on** and **Last contacted**. Changing their company or position keeps the previous company and position under **Company history**. Dates stay unknown when they haven't been provided; changing a company doesn't invent a start or end date.

## Import LinkedIn contacts

Open **Knowledge → Import** and upload your LinkedIn export ZIP. In **LinkedIn export detected**, select **Import contacts** to review `Connections.csv`; keep **Import facts** selected too if you want career facts from the same upload. The choices appear before any CSV data is read. Facts start selected and contacts start unchecked. Available email addresses are included for the people you select. Choose contacts using the checkboxes; possible duplicates need a separate match decision. Company groups are alphabetical, and people within a company sort by last name.

First and last name are kept apart as LinkedIn has them. Emoji and decorative symbols (such as ®, ™ or ★) are removed from names, companies and positions, and fancy letters such as 𝗝𝗼𝗵𝗻 become plain ones. Credentials after a last name stay. Importing again updates the first and last name of people matched by their profile link, without adding duplicates.

Review possible duplicates before saving. Selected contacts automatically bring in their companies; existing companies are reused by name, ignoring capitalization and spacing. Returning profile URLs update the same people. See [Import facts from a file](/guides/building-career-knowledge#import-facts-from-a-file) for the combined workflow. Standalone CSV upload in Directory is deferred.

## Link people to applications and interviews

In an application's **Contacts** cell, search **Find a directory contact** to pick an existing person; results sort by last name. Set their role in that application, such as Recruiter or Referral. **Add contact** asks for a first and last name and also creates a central record under the application's company.

In the interview dialog, **Who you'll meet** has the same search. Choose an existing person and enter their role for that interview. **Add a person** asks for a first and last name and creates a central contact when the interview is saved. One person can be a referral on an application and an interviewer in a particular round.

Names edited in Directory update the linked application and interview records. Removing a person from an application or interview leaves their Directory record available.

## Existing records, export and deletion

The first migration creates companies from existing applications and brings application contacts and interview participants into Directory. It merges matching names within the same company and account, preserving email addresses, phone numbers, profile links and source references. People in different accounts remain separate.

[Export everything](/guides/exporting-your-data) includes `directory.json` with companies, contacts with their first and last names, past positions, notes and the companies you watch. Account import restores them with new IDs and reconnects imported applications and interviews when both areas are selected. A contact from an older export, without separate names, comes back with its whole name as the first name.

When first and last name became separate, existing contacts kept their whole name as the first name, with no last name; nothing was guessed.

**Delete contact** removes the person, their notes and company history, and their details from linked applications and interviews. **Delete all contacts**, in the **⋯** menu, does this for every contact in your account: it also empties every application's contacts and every interview's participants. Companies and applications remain. **Delete company** keeps its contacts and applications but clears their company links; application company names remain in their history.
